### Valid PESEL Numbers
- **92082612335:** Valid male PESEL born on August 26, 1992.
- **92082612346:** Valid female PESEL born on August 26, 1992.

### Invalid PESEL Numbers
- **92082612345:** Invalid checksum digit (expected 6, got 5).
- **920826:** Too short (must be exactly 11 digits).
- **920826abcde:** Contains non-numeric characters.
