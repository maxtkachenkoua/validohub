Polish Invoice Number Helper helps developers inspect Polish data locally before it reaches forms, APIs, payment files, logs, or support tickets. The workbench focuses on deterministic browser-only checks: normalization, structure, field breakdown, masking, safe fixture generation, batch validation, and implementation boundaries.

It does not call official registries or prove that a person, company, bank account, payment code, vehicle, address, customs number, or invoice exists. Use official systems when legal or production status matters.
