# TERYT Hierarchy Explorer FAQ

## Does this contact an official service?

No. The workbench runs entirely in your browser and performs offline checks only.

## Can this confirm that a record exists?

No. It can explain structure and consistency, but it cannot confirm registration, legal status, delivery status, account ownership, or filing acceptance.

## Can I use real customer data?

The browser-only design avoids uploads, but you should still follow your organization's privacy rules. Prefer masking, local test data, and minimal copied fields.
